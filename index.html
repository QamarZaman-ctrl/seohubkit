Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • Compliant with Google AdSense, GDPR & CCPA

1. Introduction & Overview

At SEOHubKit, reachable at seohubkit.com, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by SEOHubKit and how we protect your personal data.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing & Zero Server Data Retention

All core functionalities provided on SEOHubKit—including the Meta Tag Generator, Schema Markup Generator, Keyword Density Analyzer, .htaccess Generator, Robots.txt Generator, XML Sitemap Builder, Social Card Previewer, and URL Slug Cleaner—operate completely client-side in your web browser JavaScript environment.

None of the input titles, URLs, text blocks, or configuration parameters you enter into our tools are transmitted to, stored on, or processed by external database servers. Your work remains completely private on your local machine.

3. Log Files & Standard Web Analytics

SEOHubKit follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons (Google AdSense Compliance)

Like any other website, SEOHubKit uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to seohubkit.com and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Third-Party Advertising Partners & Ad Networks

Some of the advertisers on our site may use cookies and web beacons. Our advertising partners include Google AdSense. Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on SEOHubKit, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that SEOHubKit has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

6. Third-Party Privacy Policies (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

SEOHubKit's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

Under the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), consumers have rights including:

  • The right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • The right to request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business has collected.
  • The right to opt-out of the sale of a consumer's personal data.

Since SEOHubKit does not collect or sell any personal identifying information, your data remains fully protected.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Another part of our priority is adding protection for children while using the internet. We encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and/or monitor and guide their online activity.

SEOHubKit does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.
